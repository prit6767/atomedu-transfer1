Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Atom Edu ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our web-based drafting service at atom-edu.org (the "Service"). We designed the Service to keep personal data to an absolute minimum: your drafts and any class notes live in your browser, not on our servers.

Effective date: August 1, 2026 Last updated: August 28, 2026
Short version. Your drafts, class notes, and any student information you might type stay in your browser's local storage on your device — we cannot see them and they never leave your device except when you send a prompt to be drafted. When you generate a draft, only the prompt text and the class subject you selected are sent to our AI provider. We keep aggregate counters (total drafts, unique teachers, per-model usage) and a short list of recent activity for operational and safety purposes — no student data, ever.

If you type student information into a prompt, that text is sent to the AI provider for that request, but it is not written to our storage. We strongly recommend you do not include real student names, IDs, grades, or other identifying information in prompts.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the Service: verify your eligibility, transmit your prompt to the AI provider, and return the draft to you.
  • To enforce fair use: apply per-user daily caps on high-cost model tiers, detect abuse, and prevent bypass attempts.
  • To improve the Service: aggregate counters help us decide which features to build, which models to keep, and which schools we serve.
  • To communicate about the Service: reply to your emails and, rarely, notify users of material changes or outages.
  • To comply with law: respond to lawful requests, protect our rights, and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your prompts or drafts to train any AI model, and our AI provider is under a commercial obligation not to train on your inputs. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our legal bases for processing your personal data are:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — to provide the Service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep aggregate counters, detect abuse, secure the Service, and improve reliability. We have assessed that these interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — where applicable, and you may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — to respond to lawful requests.

6. Retention

  • Local browser data (profile, drafts, classes) is kept until you clear your browser storage or use "Sign out." You control this entirely.
  • Prompt data is not retained on our servers. It exists only for the duration of a single draft request.
  • Aggregate counters are retained indefinitely; they contain no personal data.
  • Unique-teacher list retains your email until you request deletion or an administrator removes it.
  • Recent activity list retains at most the last 20 entries platform-wide and rotates as new drafts come in.
  • Rate-limit keys auto-expire after 48 hours.
  • Server logs are retained per our hosting provider's standard log policy.

7. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect information, including HTTPS everywhere, secrets stored in encrypted secret managers, minimum-necessary access, and provider-managed infrastructure hardened by Cloudflare and GitHub. Access to admin analytics is protected by a password held by us, and admin actions are gated behind that password check on every request.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal data we hold, we will notify affected users and, where applicable, regulators without undue delay.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights with respect to personal data we hold about you:

  • Access — a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — to have your data deleted.
  • Restriction / Objection — to restrict or object to certain processing, including based on legitimate interests.
  • Portability — to receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent.
  • Complaint — to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (EEA/UK residents).

To exercise a right, email us at privacy@atom-edu.org. Because most personal data lives only in your browser, deletion is fastest if you first clear your browser storage for atom-edu.org yourself; then contact us to remove your email from our unique-teacher list. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children and student data

The Service is intended for use by educators aged 18 or older. The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. No student sign-in exists. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact privacy@atom-edu.org and we will delete it promptly.

You are responsible for not entering student personal data into prompts. If you do so, you represent that you have the legal authority (including any parental consent) to process that data and that doing so complies with your school's and district's policies.

10. FERPA

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g) applies to schools that receive U.S. Department of Education funds and to the education records they maintain. Atom Edu is not itself a school or an educational agency and does not maintain student education records. Because we do not collect or store student personal information, we do not receive education records from your school.

If, in the course of using the Service, you as a school official disclose personally identifiable information from education records to us — for example, by entering it into a prompt — we act as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under 34 C.F.R. § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B) for the purpose of that single drafting request, and: (i) we perform a service the school would otherwise use its own employees to perform; (ii) we are under the direct control of the school with respect to the use and maintenance of that information; and (iii) we do not re-disclose the information or use it for any other purpose. We recommend you do not include such information in prompts.

11. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights, subject to certain exceptions:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell/share.
  • Right to delete personal information we collect from you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@atom-edu.org. We will verify your request by matching the email on file. You may designate an authorized agent by providing written permission.

12. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our processors (Cloudflare, Groq, GitHub) operate primarily from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission, for transfers of personal data out of the EEA, UK, and Switzerland.

13. Cookies and tracking

The Service does not use cookies for tracking. We do use your browser's localStorage to remember your profile and drafts on your device. We do not embed third-party analytics, advertising pixels, session-replay tools, or fingerprinting scripts.

14. Do Not Track

Because we do not track users across sites, we treat all traffic the same regardless of Do Not Track signals.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice through the Service on the next visit. Your continued use after the change constitutes acceptance.
