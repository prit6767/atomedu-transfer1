Privacy policy.

Last updated: 2026. Atom Edu is a free tool for teachers. This is the whole policy in plain English.

In one sentence: your drafts and student data live in your browser, not on our servers. What we count is drafts and unique teachers, nothing more.

What we collect

Your name, work email, subject, and any drafts you generate are stored in your browser's localStorage. When you generate a worksheet, only the prompt and class context are sent to Atom's inference backend. We do not persist those requests.

What we don't collect

No analytics. No cookies. No third-party trackers. No student names or grades ever leave your browser.

What we count (aggregate only)

Total drafts, total teachers, per-day totals, per-model totals, per-school-domain totals. Counts only. Emails are redacted to first two letters plus domain.

Model calls

Prompts go to Groq via our Cloudflare Worker. Content is not stored on our side and is not used to train any model.

Deletion

Clear your browser storage for atom-edu.org.

Children

Atom Edu is for teachers. Students never sign in.
