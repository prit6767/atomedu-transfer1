Privacy policy.

Last updated: 2026. Atom Edu is a free tool for teachers. This is the whole policy in plain English.

What we collect

Your name, work email, subject, and password (stored as-is in your own browser's localStorage — never leaves your device). Drafts you approve, students you add, and grades you enter are stored the same way — locally, on your device only. When you generate a worksheet, the interview text and topic you provide are sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which forwards them to Groq to run the GPT-OSS models. We do not persist those requests.

What we don't collect

No analytics. No cookies. No third-party trackers. No student names or grades ever leave your browser. We do not know who you are and we do not track individual accounts.

What we count (aggregate only)

Our admin dashboard tracks the total number of accounts and drafts made across the whole platform — as counts only, never as identifiable data. Emails, names, drafts, students, and grades are not visible to us.

Model calls

Prompts are sent to Groq (which runs open-weight GPT-OSS 20B and 120B). Per Groq's data policy, API prompts are not used to train models. We never send student work — only the teacher-authored spec and interview answers.

Deleting your account

Clear your browser's site data for atom-edu.org and everything is gone. There is no server-side account to delete because we never had one.

Contact

Questions: privacy@atom-edu.org.

Terms of service.

Last updated: 2026.

Who can use Atom Edu

Teachers, tutors, and educators at a real school or district (verified only by requiring a .edu or .org email address at sign-up). If you are under 18, do not create an account.

The rule

Atom drafts. You edit. Nothing goes out to students until you approve. You are the sole author of anything you distribute. Atom Edu is a drafting assistant, not a grading or communication service.

What you can do

Generate as many drafts as you want up to the daily cap of 5 assignments per teacher per day. Print them, share them, adapt them, keep them.

What you cannot do

Use the service to send communications to students or families in an automated way. Impersonate another teacher. Scrape the interface. Attempt to bypass the daily cap or the .edu/.org requirement. Use output to defame, harass, or harm.

Content ownership

Every worksheet you approve is yours. You own it. We claim no rights in your drafts, your student roster, or your grades.

Warranty

Atom Edu is provided as-is, free, with no warranty. Model output can be wrong. You are the professional in the loop — review every draft carefully.

Termination

You can stop using the service at any time by clearing your browser data. We reserve the right to disable the shared Groq backend without notice.
