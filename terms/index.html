Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 2026

In one sentence: your drafts and student data live in your browser, not on our servers. What we keep is a count of drafts and unique teachers, nothing more.

What we collect

Your name, work email, subject, and any drafts you generate are stored as they are in your own browser's local storage. They stay on your device. When you generate a document, only the prompt you typed and the class context you chose are sent to the inference backend to draft it. We do not keep those requests.

What we do not collect

No site analytics. No cookies. No third-party trackers. No student names or grades ever leave your browser. We do not know who you are and we do not track individual accounts.

What we count, in aggregate only

Our admin dashboard tracks the total number of teachers who have signed up, the total number of drafts made across the whole platform, per-day totals, per-model totals, and per-school-domain totals. These are counts only, never identifiable content. Emails are hashed for the unique-teacher count and are shown redacted, as the first two letters plus the domain, in the recent-activity list. Prompts, drafts, students, and grades are never visible to us.

Model and slideshow calls

Every draft you generate goes through a large language model provider, and slide decks you choose to export are rendered by a presentation provider. The prompt you type and the class context you attach are the only things sent, and only for that single request. Student work you upload for adaptation stays on your device unless you explicitly ask Atom to work on it. When it is sent, it is sent for that one call, is not stored on our side, and is not used to train any model, ours or a provider's.

Deletion

To delete everything, clear your browser storage for atom-edu.org. That is the whole deletion process, because there is nothing on our side to erase for you. Your account has no server-side record to remove.

Children

Atom Edu is for teachers, not students. Students never sign in. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under thirteen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date changes with it, and the change is noted on the workspace home page the first time you visit after it happens.
